## How to play

It's just simple pong, hit the ball to the other side, and try to get it past the opponents paddle!
There's 4 gamemodes to choose from:
* Easy: It's really easy
* Medium: Average difficulty, recommended to play
* Hard: Harder, but still very possible
* Impossible: don't even try

Your controls are:
(Gameplay)
* Up Arrow Key or W key (move paddle up)
* Down Arrow Key or S key (move paddle down)
* Shift (Make paddle faster)

(UI Navigation)
* Enter (select mode)
* Left Arrow Key or A key (go to option on the left)
* Right Arrow Key or D key (go to option on the right)
